🎤 Train Your Model — 100% FREE, Real In-Browser Training

No payment, no subscription. Your samples are really analysed on this device with the Web Audio API — pitch detection (autocorrelation), FFT spectrum, tempo & energy are measured from your audio, and the resulting Voice Model profile is saved in this browser (IndexedDB + localStorage). Your audio never leaves your device. When you select the model in Generate, the song's pitch, waveform, vibrato and tempo actually follow your measured profile.

📋 Model Train Karne Ka Tareeqa — Step by Step (Asaan Urdu)

  1. Step 1 — Naam likhein: neeche Model Name mein apne model ka naam likhein (masalan: Meri Awaaz).
  2. Step 2 — Samples dein: apni awaaz ke 3–5 samples upload karein ya mic se record karein — har sample 30–60 second ka ho, saaf awaaz ho, aas paas shor na ho.
  3. Step 3 — Train dabayein: Start REAL Training wala button dabayein aur progress 100% hone ka intezaar karein (internet ki zaroorat nahi, sab isi phone/browser mein hota hai).
  4. Step 4 — Model select karein: model ban jaye to woh khud select ho jata hai — warna My Models mein ja kar Use in Generate dabayein.
  5. Step 5 — Gana banayein: upar Lyrics wale hisse mein apne gana ke alfaaz likh kar Generate dabayein — gana aap ke model ki awaaz/pitch mein banega.

🖥️ Pro GPU Training (Free) — RVC-style Voice Clone

In-browser training builds a measured voice profile. For a full neural RVC-style voice-clone model, use the free GPU notebook: it runs the real RVC training pipeline (preprocess → RMVPE pitch extraction → train → index) on a free Kaggle / Google Colab GPU.

⬇ Download Kaggle / Colab Notebook (.ipynb)

  1. Prepare 3–10 minutes of clean, dry singing/voice as one dataset.zip (wav/mp3).
  2. Open kaggle.com/code (free GPU, 30 hrs/week) or colab.research.google.com → Upload the downloaded notebook.
  3. Run cells top to bottom: GPU check → install Applio/RVC → upload dataset.zip → preprocess & extract pitch/features.
  4. Train (notebook default: 200 epochs, batch 8 — lower to 100 on free GPU timeouts).
  5. Download the resulting .pth + .index model files from the notebook output.
  6. Use that model in any RVC/Applio player for full voice conversion; the in-browser profile above stays your instant, no-GPU option here.
